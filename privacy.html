Cliper Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Whose data is processed

Cliper connects only to the creator's own social media accounts. It does not collect personal data from viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

What is stored

To upload videos, Cliper stores on the creator's own computer:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the creator's accounts;
  • basic profile information returned by TikTok (user.info.basic), used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • the generated clips, their texts, and the IDs and links of uploaded videos.

How it is used

This data is used only to upload the creator's clips to the creator's own accounts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Where it is kept

All data stays on the operator's own machine. Cliper has no public server and no user database.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings under Security and permissions, then Apps and services, after which the stored token stops working and is deleted.
