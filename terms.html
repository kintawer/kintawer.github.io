Cliper Terms of Service

Effective date: 13 November 2026

What Cliper is

Cliper is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by a single creator. It finds highlight moments in recordings of the creator's own and publicly available Twitch and YouTube streams, cuts them into vertical 9:16 clips with subtitles, and uploads them to the creator's own accounts on VK, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Who may use it

Cliper is not offered to the public. The only account connected to it is the creator's own. No one else can sign up, log in or post through it.

TikTok integration

Cliper uses TikTok Login Kit only to authorize the creator's own TikTok account, and the TikTok Content Posting API only to upload videos to that account. Videos are sent to the TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator reviews, edits and publishes each one manually in the TikTok app.

Content and credit

Every clip description names the original streamer and links to their channel. Clips are short excerpts intended as highlights. If you are a streamer and want your content removed, it will be taken down on request.
